# Friction / Gap Analysis

## FRICTION: Brand Symbol vs Actual Control

Problem: A visible brand symbol may imply control, but control should not activate unless carrier, target, resistance, and faction authority checks pass.

Risk: Branding becomes cheap mind-control.

Resolution: Brand requires carrier, target filter, resistance check, activation channel, decay rule, and counterplay.

Training token: `<friction_brand_symbol_control>`

## FRICTION: Music Grid vs Physics

Problem: Rhythm wants beat alignment. Physics wants causal realism.

Risk: Rocks, bodies, and terrain feel fake if forced onto the grid.

Resolution: Beat-lock telegraphs and minor Foley. Let major collisions and topology events resolve by physics. Minor quantization may be allowed within 40 ms.

Training token: `<friction_metrical_physics>`

## FRICTION: Faction Music vs Object Identity

Problem: Church music near an object does not make the object a Church artifact.

Risk: Audio context overwrites material/entity truth.

Resolution: Music is a carrier or modifier. Identity comes from registry/entity/material state.

Training token: `<friction_music_identity>`

## FRICTION: Laban Motion vs Mass

Problem: Laban strong/light can describe motion quality, not true mass.

Risk: Visually fast/light motion causes impossible physics.

Resolution: Laban writes animation/audio/camera feel. Mass comes from volume + material_id + authored override.

Training token: `<friction_motion_mass>`

## FRICTION: Topology Resonance vs Progression Breakage

Problem: If players can crack any mountain, world progression breaks.

Risk: Sequence breaking, navmesh collapse, quest failure, terrain invalidation.

Resolution: Topology targets require authored gates: resonance signature known, instrument available, terrain target registered, route impact validated, progression lock cleared.

Training token: `<friction_topology_progression>`

## FRICTION: Cultural Grammar vs Appropriation

Problem: Real cultural grammars may inspire useful affordance models, but direct use as fantasy mechanics is risky.

Risk: Cultural extraction, shallow aestheticization, misrepresentation.

Resolution: Use abstract structural lessons unless collaboration/permission exists. Do not expose living cultural grammar as faction flavor without review.

Training token: `<friction_cultural_grammar>`
