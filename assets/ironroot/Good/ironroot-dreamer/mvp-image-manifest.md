# MVP Image Manifest — Highest-Value 20–45 for Vertex Credits
### Ranked by value; cut the line at 20, 30, or 45 depending on credits. Extends nine-month-showable.md §2 (the 8 stills fold into Tier 1).

**Value logic:** an image earns rank by serving multiple masters at once — (D) appears in the actual demo as an in-game plate or key frame, (L) anchors the LoRA style corpus, (P) press/pitch/storefront. **Credits buy only what disk lacks:** the Thornhaven era renders, E01–E08 plates, banners, and blueprints already exist in `Good\` and cost nothing but a quantize pass. Everything below is a GAP the new canon opened. Ladder law still holds: Stage-1 local drafts precede every credit spend; Sean's hand is the final gate on all of them.

**Every caption starts:** `ironroot style, hi-bit pixel art, dark fantasy, side-scroller, dithered indexed palette, nearest-neighbor, void-black ground` + negative prompts (no zodiac, no animals, no aurora, no neon, no sparkle, no gradient, no anti-alias, no watermark, no text overlay).

## Credit math (per nine-month ladder rates)
- Tier 1 (20 stills × ~30 high-res inferences budgeted, best-3 selection): fits the existing ~$250–360 Stage-3 reserve if you stop at 20.
- Tiers 2–3 extend the reserve roughly linearly (~$12–18 per additional still at the same 30-inference discipline); cutting inference budget to 15/still after the LoRA is proven is the honest way to reach 45 without doubling spend.
- Character/specimen sheets (8, 9, 10, 12, 20, 28, 37, 40) double as LoRA training reinforcement — each one that passes Sean's gate feeds the next training round, so they compound; that is why they rank above prettier vista shots.
