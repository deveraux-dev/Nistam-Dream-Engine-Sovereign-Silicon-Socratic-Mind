# Thornhaven Terrace

## Thornbell Parish

The hub district where the player cycles through *return → identify materials → craft/trade → social/economic choice → advance faction clocks → prepare next run*. Six named rooms were inherited; two are invented here to close the loop.

### Bellwright Forge

A smithy with two anvils, one cold. The forge-partner is absent; their hammer leans against the wall. *Restorer can rekindle hearth-bonds here; players earn companion loyalty by maintaining the rhythm.*

Trit: −1 (inherited debt to the craftwork tradition) / 0 (heat, hammer-falls, the work itself) / +1 (oath-weapons remember your hands)

Era-mood: Golden at opening (warm light, active forge), ancient by repeat visits (ash settling, metal memory).

Props: Hammer with maker's marks · Ledger of commissions (names crossed out) · Bell-bronze coal still warm

Sound: Hammer-ring that remembers (Anvil Covenant timbral signature)

Loop-step: Craft/Trade. Materials from The Under-Orchard → traded here → oath-weapons returned.

### Market Row

Open colonnade where Mara of the Free Guilds reprices goods under faction pressure. A chalkboard with prices struck and rewritten. The smell of cloth and salt. *This is where Social/Economic Choice lives — pay the Toll-Saints a cut, or broker a silent trade with Widow Courts.*

Trit: −1 (inherited supply-line debts) / 0 (negotiation, repricing, the deal struck) / +1 (goods move, knowledge spreads)

Era-mood: Golden (bustling, abundant), decay (fewer merchants, marked-down ledger)

Props: Bolts of black wax cloth · Clay price tokens · Folded ledger with two prices written over each other

Sound: Chain-rattle pulse cut short (Toll-Saints negotiating) with page-flip swarm underneath (Index Monks pricing memory)

Loop-step: Social/Economic Choice. Materials identified → prices negotiated → factions clock forward based on player choice.

### Witness Rail

A wide platform where testimony is inscribed on stone. Three pillars, each bearing names in three scripts: living names, toll-cut names, administratively removed names. The Index Monks maintain a small booth. *This is where players see the consequence of prior runs.*

Trit: −1 (inherited names erased) / 0 (inscription, witness-marking, the voice preserved) / +1 (name held permanent against forgetting)

Era-mood: Ancient (weathered stone), decay (more names cut than added)

Props: Chisel resting on ledger stone · Unfilled space where a name should be · Wet pages pinned to frame

Sound: Page-flip swarm into silence (Index Monks reading)

Loop-step: Return. Player enters, sees which NPCs from prior runs survived, which were cleansed. Faction clocks read here.

### Toll Gate

A narrow passage with a turnstile that counts. Toll-Sister Vey stands behind a scale. *Gap := carried − inscribed; unrecorded weight tolls.* Players must either pay coin, break the scale (Weight discipline, institution-breaking), or find a Spirit-route that bypasses. This is where most runs end: too much weight, no coin, no leverage.

Trit: −1 (inherited debt system) / 0 (weighing, negotiating, the crossing) / +1 (passage granted or denied)

Era-mood: Ancient (weathered wood), decay (scale rusted, fewer coins accepted)

Props: Bronze scale with nail marks · Toll-keeper's ledger · Chains hanging from the turnstile

Sound: Chain-rattle pulse cut short (Toll-Saints reading weight)

Loop-step: Prepare Next Run. Player exits Thornbell here, heading to The Bell Pit. Toll determines if they return to start or advance.

### Parish Shrine

A low stone chamber with a candle cairn. Altar table bare except for a cracked ceramic bowl with warm ash. *Safe rest. Companions heal here; the player's mind settles; faction pressure pauses.* But staying too long lets the Erasure creep in—the light dims.

Trit: −1 (inherited need for shelter) / 0 (rest, warmth, the vigil) / +1 (returned strength, clarity)

Era-mood: Golden (small flames, hand-made warmth), decay (ash growing cold, candles burned low)

Props: Cracked ceramic bowl with warm ash · Folded cloth without body · Blackened nail ring

Sound: Wind, or no sound (Free Graves presence—the defiant quiet)

Loop-step: Advance Faction Clocks (optional). Resting here advances time; each faction's schedule progresses. Can trigger cleanse events if clocks tick too far.

### Descent Door

A carved threshold into the stone floor, ringed with fresh gouges. Below, a spiral stair and lantern-light. *Entrance to The Under-Orchard.* A warden stone sits to one side—names can be carved here before descent, a last witness-act.

Trit: −1 (inherited underworld vows) / 0 (the choice to descend, to seek material and truth) / +1 (knowledge brought back, the grave-orchard returned to light)

Era-mood: Ancient (deep carving), decay (more names added, fewer return marked)

Props: Warden stone with half-filled name roster · Lantern left by prior visitors (some kindled, some dark) · Bell-rope marker tied to the railing

Sound: Descending minor third (grave-bell answering from below)

Loop-step: Identify Materials. Player descends to gather Bell Bronze, Bell Diamond, Grave Iron. Returns carrying materials to Bellwright Forge.

### The Toll Vault *(invented this run)*

A hidden side-chamber off Market Row, guarded by a single Toll-Saint in ceremonial armor. Locked chest inside. *This is where players negotiate directly with the Toll-Saints to reduce debt or secure passage.* Breaking in (Ash discipline, burn seals; Weight, force gates) changes faction standing permanently. Trading favor here (Thread, bargaining) advances Toll-Saints' clock.

Trit: −1 (inherited toll system enforced by institutional will) / 0 (negotiation, leverage, the hard choice) / +1 (debt reduced or transferred, one run bought)

Era-mood: Ancient (old lock mechanisms), decay (chest dented, fewer coins inside over time)

Props: Iron chest with three locks · Toll ledger (names and amounts) · Candle burned to wax-pool

Sound: Chain-rattle pulse cut short with hammer-ring underneath (Toll-Saints and Anvil Covenant in silent accord)

Loop-step: Social/Economic Choice (alternate). Players can bypass Market Row negotiation by trading directly here—different factions, different consequences.

### The Index Athenaeum *(invented this run)*

A cramped reading room off Witness Rail, shelves of thin ledger books. Index Clerk Oth works at a slanted desk, reading by candlelight. *Players bring brought-back names, Spirit echoes, recovered memory fragments here.* Oth can restore provenance (make unrecorded memories administratively real) or weaponize testimony (expose a faction's hidden debts in the ledger-court). Access costs: mercy to NPCs, or breaking Index seals.

Trit: −1 (inherited authority to decide which memories are legally real) / 0 (reading, parsing, the restoration-choice) / +1 (truth made official, or lies made law)

Era-mood: Ancient (dust on spines, hand-written marginalia), decay (books water-stained, some ledgers go missing)

Props: Desk lamp burning bone-wax · Ledger with pages marked "AUDIT" · Name fragments pinned to a felt board

Sound: Page-flip swarm into silence (Index Monks at work)

Loop-step: Return (enhanced). Players who brought evidence from prior runs can file it here, changing what future runs see at Witness Rail. Advances Index Monks' clock.

## The Bell Pit

The arena ring below Thornbell Parish, where the Toll-Saints stage public rites and the Thirteen Bells Warden counts the living. Five waves of encounters, escalating pressure; refuse a toll, and the Warden of Red Debt manifests.

### Bell Core

Central floor where players stand. Stone marked with concentric circles, worn by countless feet. *This is the witness-point.* Every strike resonates upward; the bells above answer in descending minor thirds. Thirteen bells full = Warden manifests. Breaking a bell, refusing a toll, or taking death here triggers the Red Debt path.

Trit: −1 (inherited ring, inherited witness-debt) / 0 (the stand, the choice to fight or refuse) / +1 (bell-sound recorded, world changed)

Era-mood: Golden (all bells sounding, chaos ordered into rhythm), decay (bells cracked, fewer answers to the call)

Props: Stone floor marked with blood-lines · Bell-ropes hanging slack · Toll-keeper's ledger on a brass stand

Sound: Descending minor third from above, call-and-response (Stonecalling)

Loop-step: Arena center. All encounters pass through here; this is where faction pressure becomes combat.

### Witness Balcony

Upper ring where NPCs watch. Toll-Saints in formation. Index Monks recording. Free Graves observers in shadow. Widow Courts' representatives noting inheritance. *What happens here is witnessed; it will be ledgered. This visibility is mercy and threat together.* The toll-count itself is read here, carved rung by rung into the balcony's toll-board — never floating on the air, always cut into wood.

Trit: −1 (inherited obligation to be seen, to be recorded) / 0 (the gaze, the act of witness) / +1 (memory sealed, consequence known)

Era-mood: Ancient (worn stone, many eyes over many years), decay (fewer witnesses, some seats empty)

Props: Ledger books stacked waist-high · Bell-ropes frayed · Witness-stones marked with names · The toll-board, thirteen notches deep

Sound: Page-flip swarm (Monks recording) layered under chain-rattle (Toll-Saints counting the accounts)

Loop-step: Arena support. Encounter resolution read here; faction leverage shifts based on witness-accounts.

### Boss Seal

A raised platform at the ring's edge, ringed with spell-stones. *The Thirteen Bells Warden stages here when all bells have rung thirteen times.* The stones glow faintly in the presence of a manifested boss-entity. Breaking the seal (Ash, burn; Root, reopen buried passages) changes the boss encounter completely—it becomes grounded, material, defeatable through environment rather than raw combat.

Trit: −1 (inherited summoning-pact, institutional summoning) / 0 (the calling, the gate opened or held) / +1 (boss faced and overcome, or negotiated, or fled)

Era-mood: Ancient (stones weathered), decay (seals weakening, boss harder to summon but more dangerous when it comes)

Props: Spell-stones with trit-runes carved in · Bell-rope severed at the seal · Ledger ash in a scatter

Sound: Vacuum pulse with missing echo (Hollow Astronomers' correspondence breaking as the seal fails)

Loop-step: Arena pressure-point. Manifests after five encounter waves; can be avoided by staying low on the toll-count, or weaponized by intentional escalation.

### Root Cracks

Fissures in the arena floor where earth-memory bleeds through. Spirit routes run underneath; a player can glimpse the Under-Orchard from here. *Dying in the Root Cracks opens the Spirit route into the Under-Orchard—a shortcut, a mercy, a dangerous knowledge.*

Trit: −1 (inherited earth-memory, inherited death-debt) / 0 (the crack opened, the root-path revealed) / +1 (Spirit knowledge gained, next run shorter)

Era-mood: Ancient (roots visible, moss on stone), decay (cracks widening, roots breaking through faster)

Props: Root fibers growing through stone · Bone-dust in the crevices · Spirit-mark stones (glowing faintly)

Sound: Descending contour (grave-bell answering from the Under-Orchard)

Loop-step: Arena escape-route. Players who die here (or intentionally descend) gain Spirit knowledge; their next run can skip early material-gathering.

## The Under-Orchard

Dungeon branches stemming from a central Root-Vault. Five named branches, each tied to a material type and a faction pressure. The Locked Deep Door stands at the vault's bottom—accessible only after all five branches are partially cleared.

### Root Cellar

Root vegetables in rot-wooden bins. Lantern hangs from a beam. *First branch: teaches tool-use, material identification.* Bell Bronze grows in crystal form from root-infected stone. Toll-Saints enforce a tithe here; Free Graves offer shortcuts to bypass.

Trit: −1 (inherited preservation-debt, stored memory) / 0 (harvest, identify, gather) / +1 (material in hand, knowledge of growth-pattern)

Era-mood: Golden (fresh roots, warm lantern-light), decay (bins collapsed, roots blackened, bell-bronze crystals cracked)

Props: Wooden bin with bone-dust · Bell-bronze crystal in root-vein · Ledger pinned to timber with a spike

Sound: Chain-rattle pulse (Toll-Saints counting the tithe) with wind underneath (Free Graves offering passage)

Loop-step: Material-gathering. First branch for new players; yields Bell Bronze.

### Toll Drain

Underground channel where tolls are literally drained: coins sinking in stagnant water, names written on scrolls left to rot. *Claustrophobic, stinking space.* Bell Diamond (cold, refractive, ritual-material) grows on the channel walls. Widow Courts control this branch—they trade legacy inheritance for passage.

Trit: −1 (inherited debt-flow, systematic extraction) / 0 (wading through, claiming the diamond) / +1 (material secured, debt-logic understood)

Era-mood: Ancient (stone with deep wear), decay (water filthy, diamonds clouded with residue)

Props: Scroll with name written in water-ink · Coin-pile sunken and corroded · Chain anchoring a gate

Sound: Water pressure, drowned vow (Widow Courts' signature)

Loop-step: Material-gathering. Mid-complexity branch; yields Bell Diamond. Teaches faction-economy reading.

### Bell Vein

A vertical shaft with bell-metal ore embedded in the walls. The deeper you climb, the more the stone resonates—every footstep rings faintly. *Ascent-driven space; up is toward Stonecalling, down is toward silence.* Grave Iron (heavy, sound-dampening, memory-bearing) is found at mid-depth. Anvil Covenant hammers echo here—their presence forces rhythm-based combat.

Trit: −1 (inherited resonance-debt, inherited silence-aversion) / 0 (climbing, striking stone, the echo-call) / +1 (iron claimed, your voice made matter)

Era-mood: Ancient (smooth worn stone, deep resonance), decay (stone cracked, resonance failing, iron harder to extract)

Props: Bell-metal ore with deep striations · Grave-iron wedge driven into a seam · Bell-rope marker tied at the mid-point

Sound: Hammer-ring that remembers (Anvil Covenant working the vein)

Loop-step: Material-gathering. Technical branch; yields Grave Iron. Teaches resonance-combat basics.

### Spirit Fold

A chamber where the living world folds into the Spirit world. The air shimmers; up is sideways here. *Perception-gate space—players with low clarity see only stone walls; high clarity sees the fold-geometry, the Spirit rooms layered underneath.* No material grows here, but Spirit echoes can be read—fragments of the spouse-name, prior player actions, the Shadow's earliest scars.

Trit: −1 (inherited Spirit-debt, the unseen pressure) / 0 (the gaze turned sideways, the fold traversed) / +1 (Spirit-knowledge gained, eyes opened)

Era-mood: Golden (living world bright, Spirit world luminous), decay (fold collapsing, both worlds darkening in the overlap)

Props: Cloth hanging at angles · Root fibers passing through stone without breaking it · Mirror-pool (still, non-reflective)

Sound: Vacuum pulse with missing echo (Hollow Astronomers' correspondence)

Loop-step: Spirit-route branch. Non-material, knowledge-only. Teaches death-routes; players can return here in Spirit form after death for shortcuts or hidden events.

### Grave-Mine

Deep excavation where the spouse-grave once lay. Ash and bone fragments in the earth. *Final branch; emotional weight-bearing space.* No material here, but the grave-stone marker sits at the deepest point. Names carved into it; one is blank (the spouse-name, still erased). Players can inscribe witness here, changing the marker's power—a small act of resistance against the Erasure.

Trit: −1 (inherited grief, inherited erasure-debt) / 0 (the descent, the inscription, the witness-act) / +1 (name held in stone, refusal made permanent)

Era-mood: Ancient (deep time, settling dust), decay (stone crumbling, name-marks weathered away)

Props: Grave-stone marker with blank space · Bone-dust in the form of a human outline · Chisel left behind

Sound: Descending minor third fading to silence (the grave-bell's final answer)

Loop-step: Narrative branch. Required for closure; no material but changes faction pressure and ending-paths.

### Locked Deep Door

Stone wall at the vault's bottom, ringed with seven locks (one per faction, though only five are meant for players; two remain sealed). *This canon names only what stands before it — never what is behind it.* Before the door: a stone bench, a ledger that lists everyone who refused to go deeper, and a choice-point. Players can petition factions to open the way (aligning with that faction permanently), or find a Spirit route that bypasses the lock (cost: Spirit-death and exile).

Trit: −1 (inherited boundary, inherited closure) / 0 (the choice to press or refuse) / +1 (deeper knowledge or refusal-pride)

Era-mood: Ancient (massive stone, deep settling), decay (locks weakening, door showing cracks)

Props: Seven locks (five keyed, two blank) · Ledger of refusals · Warden stone naming the five refused

Sound: Silence (the Erasure's home)

Loop-step: Dungeon gate. Marks the end of MVP material-gathering; opens deeper content post-Cleanse.

## Grey Orchard

The village where the opening occurs and where the Grey Orchard Cleanse will later consume the space. Authored lightly — this is a place to be emptied, a stage for the first event.

### Grey Orchard Commons

Central square with a well. Three houses visible, shuttered. A mason's tools lie abandoned. *This is where Morrow dies, where the Name-Shear first sounds.* The rain falls here harder than anywhere else in Thornhaven; players cannot stay long without feeling watched.

Trit: −1 (inherited peace, soon erased) / 0 (the moment of loss, the shear sounding) / +1 (refusal kindled, revenge-path opened)

Era-mood: Golden at opening (warm stone, lived-in), decay (shutters sealed, well poisoned, peace already breaking)

Props: Grave-lantern (hand-made, holding light) · Grave-stone marker with the name still whole — for now · Well with water that echoes strangely

Sound: Name-Shear at full force (the signature violence of erasure)

Loop-step: Opening event. Serves Return—player spawns here, sees the consequence, leaves changed.

### Mason's Cottage

A small workshop where the mason of Grey Orchard makes grave-markers and well-stones. Tools still warm. Ledger open on the desk—names and dates, a record of the village's dead. *An Index Monk sits here at the Grey Orchard Cleanse, reading the ledger aloud to delete it.*

Trit: −1 (inherited craft-tradition, inherited duty to record) / 0 (the inscription, the stone-work) / +1 (memory made permanent, or permanently broken)

Era-mood: Golden (workspace in use, dust motes in light), decay (tools rusted, ledger pages torn)

Props: Stone-chisel with maker's mark · Ledger of grave-names · Unfinished marker (blank where a name should be)

Sound: Hammer-ring fading into silence (the craft-tradition ending)

Loop-step: Event support. Not a loop-step itself, but context for the opening and first cleanse.

## The Three MVP Events Staged

### The Quiet Grave-Orchard (Event 1)

Graveyard surrounding the commons. Stones weathered. Paths where Morrow walked. *This event stages the opening death and the spouse-name erasure.* Players witness the Name-Shear, feel the Erasure's grip, and must choose: grieve and leave, or pursue immediate vengeance (failing—combat tutorial loss teaches this path is wrong). Full beat-by-beat canon lives in *the-vowless-knife.md* §VI.

Loop-step: Opening event, Return. Sets all faction clocks to their starting position; death here grants a Spirit route into the Under-Orchard.

### The Desperate Camp (Event 4)

An armory pretending to be a village. Canvas tents. Cages for prisoners. Hidden altar where the Warden of Red Debt accepts new debtors. *Magenta wrongness nobody names—the corruption is present but the air refuses to speak it.* Player dies here scripted; respawn in the Spirit realm opens the second act. Frame-by-frame staging lives in *frames-first-loop.md*.

Loop-step: Mid-loop event, Prepare Next Run (inverted—this run ends; next one begins). Sets faction pressure climbing.

### The Spirit Forest Mirror (Event 5)

Same geometry as the living forest above, but emptied of warmth. Every sound echoes inverted (descending calls rise; silence screams). *The player walks here as a Spirit, learning death-routes and reading their Shadow's earliest scars.* Exit by climbing the inverted bell-vein or by refusing the route entirely (stays dead; next cycle harder).

Loop-step: Post-death event, Spirit-route exclusive. Teaches non-combat problem-solving; Spirit knowledge carries forward to next cycle.
