## STALK I: THE GRAVE-ORCHARD ROAD (Silence Before Shear)
**Running Clock: 0–30 seconds**

Overhead silence. Grave-orchard sprawls in monochromatic void-black; weathered headstones scattered like fallen teeth. Rain falls in single-pixel droplets, no splay, each one a deliberate mark in the dark. The player-silhouette stands at the road's mouth, shoulders squared, hands empty. The beloved grave (Morrow's) in the middle distance, lit by the last work of a hand-built lantern: a single bell-bronze glow the size of a fist.

**PALETTE** void-black, grave-iron, bell-bronze, ash-grey soil  
**INPUT** Hold (stand sentinel); then Push (advance toward the light)  
**SOUND** The world's held breath; a single faint wind-note ascending, then cut  
**TIME** 6 ticks silence, then 12 ticks slow walk  
**LEDGER** NONE — opening stance is declared, not yet acted  
**CAPTION** ironroot style, overhead grave-orchard, rain-dark, hand-lit lantern in distance, grief stance

### [L02] SHOT
Mid-approach. The lantern-light catches the near edges of three carved grave-markers. The player's shadow falls SHORT of the stone—the light behind them, not above. Footprints do not stay in mud; each step dissolves into void as if the road has no memory. The rain hisses softly but does not sound like water; it sounds like paper being quietly torn.

**PALETTE** void-black, bone-pale stone edge, bell-bronze occlusion, shadow-black silhouette  
**INPUT** Push (steady approach, no dodge, no hurry—the movement of someone returning to work)

## STALK II: FIRST TEACHING — ROOTED DESERTERS (Three Verbs in One Breath)

**Running Clock: 30–90 seconds**

### [L06] SHOT
The grey figure retreats. The rain returns. The player is now standing in a cleared space at the road-fork; three paths branch ahead. The lantern sways—the cloth inside it is BURNING now. The Name-Shear's sound fades into a distant, rhythmic clanging (bells, struck in descending intervals, coming from the far direction). The player's breath is visible—cold air, visible work, visible presence. A new sound arrives: the shuffle of earth, the creak of roots. From the left path, a silhouette emerges: slouched, limb-lengths wrong, moving like something reassembling itself. An **ash-warped debtor**—human-shaped, but with roots growing THROUGH the joints, cracked ceramic visible under grey cloth, bound in tomb-chains. This is what debt looks like when it becomes flesh.

**PALETTE** void-black ground, grave-iron chains, ash-grey cloth, root-brown seeping from seams, witness-crimson on exposed ceramic  
**INPUT** The player must CHOOSE: Hold (ready, wait for the tell), Push (advance and strike first), or Pull (retreat, learn before committing)  
**SOUND** Creeping root-scrape, an irregular rhythm like a stopped heartbeat; the distant bell-clanging continues underneath, a countdown  
**TIME** 18 ticks of approach, 3 ticks of decision-freeze  
**LEDGER** [ENCOUNTER: Ash-Warped Debtor — Road-Fork Toll Debt]  
**CAPTION** shambling rooted form emerges, chains audible, ceramic cracks glow faintly, ground-mist pools at bound feet

### [L07] SHOT
**The player HOLDS.** Standing ground. Watching. The debtor's movement reveals a rhythm—shamble-pause-drag-pause—mechanical, like a ledger being read aloud. It is a RHYTHM THEY CAN PARRY. This is the first teaching: **the world announces when it strikes.** The debtor's head jerks toward the player; the Name-Shear calls AGAIN from the grove behind it, but quieter, more distant. The debtor flinches at the sound. It is ALSO suffering from what chases. A moment of recognition—both are being hunted by the same administration. This frame teaches **mercy as strategic information.**

**PALETTE** void-black, grave-iron chains, the debtor's silhouette fractionally turning, distant bell-pulse visible as red-orange flicker through the trees  
**INPUT** Hold (the player says nothing; the stance itself is the communication)  
**SOUND** The debtor's rhythm breaks momentarily—it has sensed a PRESENCE. A response-call from the debtor (a low, grinding moan) rises and meets the distant bells. Call-and-response, not between player and debtor, but between debtor and the chasing ledger  
**TIME** 12 ticks of hold, the debtor's rhythm destabilizes  
**LEDGER** [PARRY-TEACH: Rhythm Observed — Ledger-Debt Marked]  
**CAPTION** two-point standoff, debtor's rhythm visible as pose-sequence, bells answer from distance, shared prey understands

### [L08] SHOT
**The debtor attacks.** It lunges with the shamble-drag motion; its chains swing wide. BUT the player SAW the rhythm coming—the PARRY is simply the opposite of the shamble-drag. At the moment the debtor's weight lands, the player is NOT there. The knife edges upward, past the chains. This is not a counter—it is a **refusal to be where the blow expects.** The debtor stumbles, recovering into a second rhythm: the drag now happens without the shamble. Its tempo increases. The distant bells ACCELERATE. The countdown is speeding up.

**PALETTE** void-black, grave-iron chains catching bell-bronze light, a wash of crimson-witness as the knife passes the debtor's silhouette  
**INPUT** Pull (the parry is a withdrawal, not an advance; the knife creates space)  
**SOUND** Chain-rattle, desynchronized; a beat missed in the bell-toll; the second bell-toll is FASTER than the first, a new urgency in the call  
**TIME** 6 ticks for the parried attack, 3 ticks for the recovery, 4 ticks for the accelerated rhythm to establish  
**LEDGER** [PARRY: Debtor-Lunge Evaded — Rhythm Learned]  
**CAPTION** sideswipe parry through chains, crimson flare, debtor off-balance, bells grow urgent

### [L09] SHOT
**The player responds.** The kukri comes forward now—not as fury, but as the completion of the rhythm the debtor started. It is Push: advancing into the space the debtor abandoned. The blade cuts the chains, not the flesh. Chains fall away. For a moment, the debtor is released—still rooted in its joints, still bound by its own debt, but the EXTERNAL chains are broken. The debtor staggers backward, and for a single frame its face (still obscured by grave-cloth) tilts DOWN—a gesture of acknowledgment. **It chooses not to continue.** The knife is a tool of execution, but here it severed debt, not life.

**PALETTE** void-black, chains in mid-fall (bright grave-iron), the debtor's form suddenly lighter, a wash of crimson-witness at the cut point  
**INPUT** Push (this is the only aggressive input, and it is PURPOSEFUL—completing what the rhythm demanded, not what rage demanded)  
**SOUND** Chains cut—a clean transient, high-frequency scrape. A response from the debtor: a low, releasing moan (descending, peaceful). The distant bell-toll STOPS. A single deep bass note (the grave-bell again, but THIS TIME resolving upward)  
**TIME** 3 ticks for the advance, 2 ticks for the cut, 6 ticks of held aftermath  
**LEDGER** [MERCY: Debtor-Chains Severed — Debt Paused, Not Erased]  
**CAPTION** knife cuts chains, not flesh, debtor released, descending moan becomes ascending acknowledgment

### [L10] SHOT
The debtor moves past the player, back toward the grave-orchard. It does not flee; it RETURNS. The Name-Shear's rhythm resumes, fainter, following the debtor back into the darkness. The player is alone on the road again. The lantern behind them still burns—Morrow's grave still unbroken, at least for now. But the chains the player cut? They are written in the ledger. Somewhere, an Index Monk records: "The Vowless Knife broke a toll debt on Road-Fork Alpha, year unknown, time-of-fall uncalculated." The player must EARN the right to say why they did it.

**PALETTE** void-black, the debtor's silhouette receding, grave-iron chains still visible on the ground, bell-bronze lantern-glow warm but dimming  
**INPUT** None (breathing space; the player walks forward, toward the Camp)  
**SOUND** Chains settling into silence. The wind picks up—no longer the sound of NAME-SHEAR, but ordinary grief. A single bird-call (not an animal; the sound is METALLIC, like a bell struck once) from far ahead  
**TIME** 12 ticks of stillness, then footsteps resume  
**LEDGER** [WITNESSED: Mercy Ledger +1 — Debtor-Mercy Margin Increases]  
**CAPTION** debtor retreats carrying new knowledge, chains fall to void-ground, uncut flesh becomes witness

## STALK III: THE DESPERATE CAMP OUTSKIRTS (Faint Wrongness)

**Running Clock: 90–140 seconds**

### [L11] SHOT
The road widens. Ahead: a camp sprawls at the forest's edge, half-built, half-abandoned. Tents made of old sail-cloth, grey-white and salt-stained. A makeshift wall of stacked stones and furniture (beds, chairs, a pulpit?). Smoke rises, but the smoke is faintly MAGENTA—the color of wrong air, air that remembers being inside something. The player has seen this color once before (in a previous life, a previous run, a place they cannot now recall). A sign is posted at the camp's mouth: raw wood, marked with a single nail and a word carved in an unknown hand—a debt-mark, like what the Ledger Church uses. Three figures move inside the camp-boundaries: two stoking fires, one standing at the center, directing work with long arm-sweeps. The standing figure wears iron and cloth; their shoulders carry the weight of COMMAND.

**PALETTE** void-black ground, grey-white sail-cloth, bell-bronze smoke, faint magenta wrongness in the air, grave-iron debt-mark, the warlord's silhouette in dark iron  
**INPUT** Push (advance to the camp-edge); Pull (slow approach, learning)  
**SOUND** Voices calling work-rhythms (not words, but tones: up-tone for *do this*, down-tone for *stop*). A hammer-strike, ringing bell-bronze. The echo carries longer than physics should allow  
**TIME** 24 ticks of approach, voice-fragments layering  
**LEDGER** [SIGHT: Camp Acknowledged — Faction Unknown, Warlord-Form Observed]  
**CAPTION** ragtag camp, magenta-tinted smoke, iron-clothed warlord directing, debt-marked gate

### [L12] SHOT
**Up close at the boundary.** The camp is not a refuge; it is a STOCKPILE. Stacked arms (swords, axes), pressed cloth bundles (ledgers?), barrels marked with runes (not written language; impressed, like seal-marks from the Ledger Church). The warlord turns directly toward the player. Their face is obscured by an old helm—cracked, once-execution-grade (a nail crown, Ledger Church issued). But the eyes visible inside are SHARP, aware, measuring. The warlord reads the player's stance (Root-Pruner posture) and nods—a slow recognition. "I know what you WERE." The magenta smoke deepens. Behind the warlord, through the tent-openings, the player glimpses something: a figure hung from tent-rope, motionless, marked with ash and cloth. A PRISONER, or a CORPSE, or both.

**PALETTE** void-black, grave-iron arms-stack, bell-bronze seal-marks on barrels, the warlord's silhouette iron-dark, magenta smoke now almost VISIBLE as vapor, the hung figure in ash-grey and white cloth  
**INPUT** Hold (stand in conversation; this is not a fight yet)  
**SOUND** The warlord speaks in tones, not words: a low growl that ascends into a question-mark at the end. The hammer-strikes from the workers STOP—the whole camp listening. Magenta smoke carries a faint sound-component, like a sustained whisper  
**TIME** 6 ticks of warlord's address, 6 ticks of held response-time  
**LEDGER** [SPEECH: Warlord-Recognition — "I Know What You Were"] — but WHAT does this recognition buy?  
**CAPTION** warlord-helm, nail-crown, eyes bright in shadow, prisoner-shape glimpsed, ash and rope

### [L13] SHOT
The player's response is SILENT KNOWLEDGE. They do not answer in words. Instead, their stance shifts—a micro-movement only a Root-Pruner would recognize. The warlord's eyes narrow. "You did *that* work for us once. Before you broke the tool." (A reference to choosing not to use the kukri.) The second worker—a thin figure with a ledger-tablet tied to their chest—steps forward. "She wants to hire you," they say, pointing at the warlord. "To clear the Camp's debt. One last contract." The magenta wrongness INTENSIFIES. The player's vision blurs for a frame; when it clears, the world is SHIFTED—not obviously, but the tent-arrangement has changed, the prisoners-or-corpses now VISIBLE all around the camp's interior. This is a SHIMMER. This is the Spirit realm bleeding through.

**PALETTE** void-black, sudden SHIFT-tint (an overlay of sickly magenta-amber), the camp re-illuminated in spirit-vision, the prisoner-shapes now OBVIOUS, all of them  
**INPUT** Pull (the player steps BACK from the shimmer, refusing to let it pull them into the Spirit side yet)  
**SOUND** The warlord's voice CONTINUES as if speaking across a gap: "Will you take the work, Knife?" Her question-mark tone descends into a demand-tone. The prisoners (if they are alive) begin a low moan in unison. The ambient whisper-sound RISES.  
**TIME** 3 ticks for the shimmer-arrival, 6 ticks held in the shifted vision, 3 ticks for the pull-back  
**LEDGER** [VISION: Spirit-Bleed Witnessed — Camp Debt Scar Visible]  
**CAPTION** magenta shimmer, spirit-camp revealed, prisoners visible in doubled geometry, warlord's offer carries weight across the veil

### [L14] SHOT
The player refuses WITH THE BODY, not words. They turn—a single pivot. But the turn reveals that the camp has MOVED. The position has shifted. The exit-path that was there is no longer there. The world has LOCKED the player in. The warlord's laugh is a hammer-strike—"Too late for refusal." The moment BREAKS. The combat timing STARTS. The warlord draws a pitted iron maul. The two workers step BACK and to the sides (they will not fight; they are witnessing). The prisoners continue their unison moan. The magenta smoke THICKENS until it is almost solid. The player is now in **THE OVERWHELM**—a single player, outnumbered not by bodies but by CONSEQUENCE WEIGHT. The game tells the player plainly: "You die here. This is the teaching."

**PALETTE** void-black, magenta smoke now nearly OPAQUE, the warlord's silhouette wielding iron, the imprisoned shapes behind creating a WALL of grief-sound  
**INPUT** The player CAN fight here. Edge players can parry. Weight players can stagger. But the INPUT will not matter. The warlord has momentum, position, and NUMBERS (not of bodies; of unpaid debts). The camera CLOSES in—a shift toward Knife 2D intimate distance  
**SOUND** The moan becomes a chant, a rhythm that no individual player can match alone. The warlord's hammer-strike RINGS against the player's guard (if they guard). The bells from the grave-orchard are now SILENT—the Name-Shear has gone FAR away  
**TIME** 12 ticks of the impossible fight-opening  
**LEDGER** NONE — this is a cutscene combat, not yet a recorded kill  
**CAPTION** player locked in camp, warlord draws maul, magenta choir of prisoners, outnumbered by weight-of-consequence, knife-vision closes in

## STALK IV: THE SCRIPTED DEATH (World's Weight)

**Running Clock: 140–160 seconds**

### [L15] SHOT
The warlord's maul SWINGS. It is a slow, powerful move—not fast, but HEAVY, carrying all the weight of the camp's debt on its edge. The player COULD dodge; the rhythm is visible, teachable. But the camera LOCKS—this is cinematic now. The dodge does not work because the WORLD will not allow it. Every move the player makes, the warlord's maul reaches further. It is not about stats; it is about POSITION. The player is surrounded by the moaning prisoners, their chains, the wall of grief-sound. There is no space to evade. The maul comes DOWN.

**PALETTE** void-black, magenta smoke, the maul's iron catching bell-bronze light (wrongly, impossibly bright), crimson WASH as the world-weight lands  
**INPUT** The player presses every button they know. Hold (brace for impact), Push (try to counter), Pull (try to evade). None of it WORKS. The game is explicitly refusing input-validity  
**SOUND** The maul-strike comes DOWN and doesn't hit sound—it hits SILENCE. A complete erasure of audio for exactly 6 ticks. Then: the crash. It is MASSIVE, physically impossible, a resonance that shakes the void-black ground. The prisoners' moan STOPS  
**TIME** 3 ticks for the swing, 6 ticks of silence (the impact), then aftermath  
**LEDGER** NONE — yet  
**CAPTION** world-weight descends, maul impossible-bright, player surrounded by consequence-walls, the strike erases sound

### [L16] SHOT
**DARKNESS.** The player's vision closes. Not black; *nothing*. Sensory void. For exactly 12 ticks, there is no game-state. The player exists in a non-space. Then: a single bell-tone sounds. A grave-bell, descending, the same pitch as the name-shear, but LOWER, accepted, grieved. The vision OPENS. The player is INSIDE the void-black. Floating. Transparent. They can see their own bones drawn in grave-iron ink under the skin. The hands are outlined in white—no flesh-color, just OUTLINE. This is the Spirit side. This is death. And it is SILENT now. The magenta wrongness is GONE. In its place: a different wrongness. The cold of administration.

**PALETTE** pure void-black, the player's bones drawn in grave-iron outline, hands in bone-white outline, silence-presence, the absence of magenta is conspicuous  
**INPUT** None (the player cannot move in the spirit-void)  
**SOUND** One bell-tone, held for 12 ticks, then SILENCE  
**TIME** 24 ticks total (the erasure and the wake)  
**LEDGER** [DEATH: The Overwhelm Claimed — Knife Spirited, Debt-Marked] — the ledger WRITES. The player is now a permanent receipt in the system  
**CAPTION** spirit-void, player transparent, bones outlined, grave-bell tolls welcome, silence-administration

## STALK V: SPIRIT FOREST MIRROR (The Dead's Knowledge)

**Running Clock: 160–210 seconds**

### [L17] SHOT
The void RESOLVES into geometry. The player is now looking at the **Desperate Camp** AGAIN, but inverted. The same tents, same positions, SAME PRISONER SHAPES—but they are all EMPTY now. The bodies are gone. Only the OUTLINES remain, drawn in black wax on grey cloth. The roofs are inverted; the smoke falls UPWARD. The warlord's position is marked by a SHADOW that is NOT moving—a permanent scar in the landscape, a mark of where violence happened. The player understands: they are seeing the Spirit-memory of this place. This is what the city remembers. And the city is making them WALK through it.

**PALETTE** void-black, the camp inverted (lighter ground, darker sky), grey wax-mark outlines of prisoner shapes, the warlord's position SCAR'd in deep void-black  
**INPUT** Pull / Push / Hold — movement is possible now, but it is SLOW, weighted, as if moving through grief-air  
**SOUND** No sound at first. Then: footsteps. The player's own footsteps, echoing from nowhere and everywhere  
**TIME** 18 ticks of re-orientation  
**LEDGER** [SPIRIT-PASSAGE: Dead-Road Mirror Entered — Camp-Scar Visible] — the ledger can reach even here  
**CAPTION** inverted spirit-camp, wax-mark prisoner outlines, scar-shadow of warlord-position, echo-steps on void-ground

### [L18] SHOT
The player begins to MOVE through the Spirit camp. As they walk, they understand the GEOGRAPHY. There is a central tent—larger than the others. Inside (they can see through the wax-marked walls), there is a ROOM. Not a war-room; a LIVING room. A hearth, cold. A bed, empty. A shelf with personal items: a small carved stone (the shape of a name), a folded letter, a needle threaded with ash-grey cord. This is not part of the warlord's camp. This is OLDER. This is a *previous* room, existing UNDER the camp. The Spirit side has stacked the geometries. The player is walking through PALIMPSEST—the dead city layered beneath the living one.

**PALETTE** void-black, grey wax-marks, the hearth inside the central tent rendered in deeper void-black (it is COLDER), the personal items drawn in bone-white, ancient ink  
**INPUT** Push (the player walks into the central tent, compelled; it is a Pull dressed as a Push)

**TIME** 9 ticks of approach, 6 ticks of entering the inner room  
**LEDGER** [DISCOVERY: Palimpsest-Room Entered — Pre-Camp Layer Exposed]  
**CAPTION** spirit-camp's inner tent, wax-walls transparent, ancient hearth and personal room below, layered geometry revealed

### [L19] SHOT
The player kneels (or stands; the posture shifts into REVERENCE without input). They read the letter. It is unsigned, but the handwriting is FAMILIAR. It is *their* handwriting—the KNIFE'S old handwriting, from before they buried the tool. The letter reads: "I have cut what I was asked to cut. Today, I cut the name of [BLANK]. Tomorrow, they will ask me to cut a thousand more. I will refuse. I will take what I have done and I will refuse to do it again. I am putting the knife down." The date on the letter is SEVENTEEN YEARS AGO. This is a PROPHECY the player has already lived. They have ALREADY chosen refusal. They have ALREADY buried this moment. But here, in the Spirit Forest, time is not linear. The player understands: their PAST refusal is still rippling forward. It is STILL HAPPENING.

**PALETTE** void-black, the letter rendered in bone-white ink, the BLANK space where the name was supposed to be is now CRIMSON—a scar, a refusal written in blood-light  
**INPUT** The player reads (cinematic hold)  
**SOUND** As the player reads, the handwriting SOUNDS—a voice that is their own voice, from seventeen years ago, reading the letter aloud. The voice is calm, resolute, and utterly grief-stricken. It is the sound of someone who has already decided to die  
**TIME** 18 ticks to read the whole letter  
**LEDGER** [WITNESS: The Knife's Own Refusal — Past-Prophecy Confirmed]  
**CAPTION** ancient letter in spirit-room, bone-ink and crimson-blank, player's own voice reads past refusal, seventeen-year echo

### [L20] SHOT
The player looks UP from the letter. The spirit-room SHIFTS. The walls are no longer wax; they are now PAPER. The entire room is made of ledger-pages, stacked, pinned, overwritten. They can READ the pages. Each one is a NAME. A person the knife ERASED, seventeen years ago. Hundreds of names. They are not dead; they are ADMINISTRATIVELY UNHAPPENED, which is worse. And the player is INSIDE the tower, part of its structure now. To move is to disturb the names. To stand is to agree that this weight should stay.

**SOUND** The breathing sound is a CHOIR of voices—each name, breathing as if still alive. The effect is UNSETTLING, beautiful, horrifying. As the player breathes in, the voices breathe in. As they breathe out, the voices breathe out. They are SYNCHRONIZED  
**TIME** 12 ticks held in the breathing tower  
**LEDGER** [REMEMBRANCE: Tower of Erased Names Recognized — Knife's Past Culpability Scar'd]  
**CAPTION** spirit-tower of ledger-pages, each name hand-written, breathing synchronized, player surrounded by administrative ghosts

### [L21] SHOT
A SOUND from outside the tower. The bell-tone again—but this time, it is DIFFERENT. It is ASCENDING. A response-call. The Grave-Orchard's lantern is RINGING from the world of the living. **INPUT** The player is PULLED back (no choice; pure cinematic)

**TIME** 12 ticks of the chord-rise  
**LEDGER** [RESURRECTION: The Grave-Lantern Calls — Return to Body Initiated]  
**CAPTION** ledger-pages fall in paradox-motion, tower breathes final breath, ascending bell-chord shatters spirit-geometry

### [L22] SHOT
**RETURN.** Blinding sensory return. The player's eyes open. They are lying in the void-black soil of the grave-orchard, rain on their face.
