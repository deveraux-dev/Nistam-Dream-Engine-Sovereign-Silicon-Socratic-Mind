# Executive Summary

Dirge of Ironroot can be treated as a playable resonance compiler:

- Factions are musical operating systems.
- Brands are executable symbolic contracts.
- Instruments are interfaces into entity, material, memory, zone, and topology state.
- Raga-inspired modes define lawful emotional, temporal, and resonance grammar.
- Tala-like cycles define when actions become lawful.
- Forge steganographic glyphs encode visible lore, human memory, and machine behavior.
- Theory-to-engine priors stabilize generation but never own final runtime truth.

## Central mechanic

The bard is not a conventional spellcaster. The bard learns how reality is notated, then performs phrases that resolve against validated runtime targets.

```text
SongPhrase + Carrier + TargetSignature + RegistryAuthority + ValidatorPass = RuntimeEffect
```

## Main compiler rule

```text
Theory proposes.
Registry decides.
Validator gates.
```

## Strong use cases

1. Rhythm-combat boss fights where enemy attacks are authored as score events.
2. Faction-branded creatures, towns, terrain, and materials.
3. Mountain cracking through topology resonance.
4. Procedural dungeons structured as musical forms.
5. Cultural/faction glyph pages that compile into behavior trees, AI priorities, audio motifs, and shader/VFX states.
6. Public-domain/reference-derived asset pipelines that track provenance and avoid hallucinated physical/material inference.

## Non-negotiable guardrail

Music, color, movement, glyphs, and cultural theory are not magic truth. They are candidate generators. Final execution belongs to registered data, deterministic simulation, and explicit validators.
