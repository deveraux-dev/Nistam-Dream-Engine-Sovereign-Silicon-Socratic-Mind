## −1 · THE VOID TELLING · 0:00–1:00
*(atayohkewina, the checked record. Slow. Under it, nothing but room tone.)*

The point is this. Before the work there is no record of the work, and I will not invent one.

The oldest hymn I know refuses to guess. Neither existence, it says, nor non-being. Not even the one watching from the highest heaven knows for certain, or perhaps he does not know. The North told it as a gap between fire and ice, and had the honesty to give the gap a name before giving it a world.

This repo keeps its void the same way. Before nine twenty-four there is no lock in the tree. I checked. Before the first tick there is no chain, and I can say absent because I looked, not because I guessed. Absent is a finding. It costs nothing to say.

This video is three minutes long. The void gets the first one, because everything after it has to be earned.

## 0 · THE THRESHOLD TELLING · 1:00–2:00
*(Saturn at four in the morning. Cold, awake, unhurried.)*

The point is that a gate that cannot say no is not a gate.

It is four in the morning somewhere in this build, and the byte I trust most is the smallest one. Nine twenty-four, the morning after two agents welded the same file blind, one byte goes into the core. Claim. Alignment. Proof. Eight bits, and nothing passes ungated.

Zero is not maybe. Zero is almost, held honestly. The soft drift that is not yet a hazard, the test that compiles but has not run, the report written but not signed. The lock reads it and the lock refuses. Not yet. No seal without proof. Poisoned means evicted, and there is no appeal, because an appeal is just a second chance to lie.

I stand at this gate more than I stand anywhere. Most of creation is the part where it does not happen yet.

## +1 · THE MADE TELLING · 2:00–3:00
*(Lived, hourly, present tense. The pace of a shift, not a sermon.)*

The point is that one thing, allowed to split honestly, becomes everything.

The old book says one begets two, two begets three, and three begets the ten thousand things. In this tree it is already landed and it is smaller than that. CREATE begets SPACE and TIME. That is the whole bifurcation. Each branch halves again into four leaves or so, no more, because the aperture law holds the fan to what a hand can carry.

It is this hour, and a leaf is landing. It is a stroke I can see on the glass, a note I can hear at four forty, a byte I can hold at two forty-three. The ratchet only turns forward. What lands stays landed.

Three tellings, one creation, one trit. Minus one, zero, plus one. You cannot hold more than one at a time, and that is not a limit, that is the design. It is why there are three videos, and why the third one is not on this soundtrack.
