GRAMMAR NOTE (Sean, this turn): the spec's form was right and the first substitution was wrong.
"Sentinel SIMD Kill" and ".zeroize()" are VERBS, and screen B is the verb bed - it may carry only
vectors. "arbitration" is a noun, it is substrate, it belongs on screen A with the steel. The
corrected line keeps the measured number AND the imperative form: ARBITRATE / ALLOCATE / REPUDIATE.
Both laws bind at once. A true number in the wrong part of speech still breaks the collision, because
the adjective in the viewer's head only renders when A supplies mass and B supplies direction.
If a zeroize bench ever lands, its frame reads ZEROIZE <n> ns - verb first, number second, never a
noun on the right-hand screen.
