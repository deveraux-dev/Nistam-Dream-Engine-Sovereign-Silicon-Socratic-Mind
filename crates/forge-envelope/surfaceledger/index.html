The forge WHAT THIS PLACE IS

Not a company, not a product. A one-man forge where trade discipline gets compiled into machine law: balanced ternary instead of tokens, integer ticks instead of wall clocks, evidence chains instead of trust. Everything is #![no_std] Rust, and nothing gets claimed without a receipt.

The pieces come off the bench with names — the S13 sieve, the ephemeral envelope, the weaver, the thirteen sentinels. They're built to outlive my opinion of them.

On the bench CURRENT WORK

Surface Ledger — an inspection auditor that can't lie about what it saw. A photo of corroding steel goes in; Gemini renders a schema-locked verdict; the disposition folds into a rolling SHA-256 chain and the raw data is wiped. A backdated log or a swapped photo breaks every seal after it.
