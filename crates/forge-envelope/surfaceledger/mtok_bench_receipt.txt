WHAT THIS SETTLES
The token throughput figure reproduces at 2.3 to 2.8 Mtok/s single core across independent runs.
The claimed 856.16 Mtok/s single core and 6.42 Gtok/s aggregate do not appear in any run.
The gap is roughly 311x. Those two figures are withdrawn.

The 1.17 ns/token figure was a real measurement of a direct L1 table lookup, not of token
generation. The terminal summary inside Proofs.mp4 states this distinction itself: L1 table
lookups versus FP16 and BF16 LLM inference. The number was correct and its later use was not.

WHAT SURVIVES, AND WHY IT IS STRONGER
Ampere dispatch planning returned 874.13 Mplans/s and 1.14 ns to the digit, twice, on separate
runs. PCIe staging held inside 1.2 percent. The L2 grid held inside 6.5 percent. Token routing
held inside its band. A deterministic engine that reproduces under re-measurement is the claim
worth making, and it is the claim the receipts actually support.
