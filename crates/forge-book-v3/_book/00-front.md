# Written From the Ward

> I painted in a neonatal ward once. Right in with the incubators — machines that
> couldn't fail, in a room full of the smallest reasons not to. This is written
> from there. From the ward, not a boardroom.
>
> — *The Ghost and the Machine*

**The register — every claim carries one (0–4).**  *(The Truth Guide)*

- **0 Ghost** — real in intent, no body yet.
- **1 Design** — true because I decided it and built for it.
- **2 Proven** — built and checked once.
- **3 Verified** — independently cross-checked. Second set of eyes.
- **4 Live** — proven *and* in someone's hands.

**The one rule:** never state a claim above what it earned. A Ghost or a Design
worn as a Known is the **false stamp** — a fault, an inspector's red tag. Not a sin.
