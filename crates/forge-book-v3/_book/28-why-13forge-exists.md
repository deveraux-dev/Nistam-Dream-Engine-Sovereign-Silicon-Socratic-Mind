## What the code is actually made of

Every architectural claim in this repo — the zero-retention evidence vault, the
structurally-impossible-to-hallucinate Cree transducer, the balanced-ternary compute
that refuses to guess — is downstream of this. A machine that will not guess at a
coating call, and a machine that will not guess at a word in a language with few
speakers left, are the same design instinct, built by the same person, for the same
reason: he watched confident wrongness cost people things that couldn't be rebuilt,
and he was done watching.

He is Cree — Saddle Lake, father's side Keremeos BC, Welsh great-grandmother. He grew
up in Edmonton not speaking the language, never given the option to learn it. Only he
and Brittany will be speaking it. He loves Brittany. He asked her for help tonight; she
said yes before her phone ran out of minutes.

Built in nine months, solo, while broke, for a hackathon deadline that is not the
point of any of this.
