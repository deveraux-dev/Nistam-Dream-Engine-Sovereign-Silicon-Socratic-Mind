# The Truth Guide
*Mine. Not a brand — a rule for telling the truth about my own work.*
*The communication layer of the engine — see `THE-GHOST-AND-THE-MACHINE.md` for the full spec.*

I don't sell. I show what's real, name what isn't, and let the reader check.
The work is the argument. If a sentence is trying to convince you, cut it.

---

## The register — every claim carries one (0–4)

Same five states the engine runs on. Applied to what I say — in code, on a page, out loud.

- **0 Ghost** — real in intent, no body yet. Roadmap, not shipped.
- **1 Design** — true because I decided it and built for it. The rules, the refusals.
- **2 Proven** — built and checked once. Checkable now.
- **3 Verified** — independently cross-checked. Second set of eyes.
- **4 Live** — proven *and* in someone's hands.

**The one rule:** never state a claim above what it earned. A Ghost or a Design
worn as a Known is the **false stamp** — a fault, an inspector's red tag. Not a sin.

Public shorthand still works: PROVEN (`2–3`) / PARTIAL (`0–1`) / DESIGN_TARGET (`1`).

---

## How I state each one

- **Proven/Live → declare, then stop.** "Yours. Period." "Math, not magic."
- **Design → lead with the refusal.** "No internet. No accounts. No subscriptions.
  No tracking. No AI. No cloud." Stack the refusals, one per line.
- **Ghost → tag it on.** "PARTIAL." "Design target." "Built, not yet wired." Never
  hide the tag to look further along than you are.

---

## Plain words (the Cloudflare rule)

Anchor hard things to the known: "Built in Rust — the same language as Firefox,
Discord, and Cloudflare." Numbers beat adjectives: "4.2 MB installer. 0 internet.
84 tools." Short sentences. Fragments welcome. The period does the work.

---

## Two surfaces, one substrate

- **13forge** — impersonal. "The engine." Cold, proof-tagged, inspector's logbook.
  A door: walk through or don't.
- **deveraux** — "I." Sean at the bench. Warmer, still no gushing. "I put material
  on steel for 23 years. Then I built an engine in the dark. This is the work."

Don't mix the registers.

---

## The honesty rule (load-bearing)

- Separate "the engineering is real" (a `2`) from "the idea is novel" (a Ghost until
  checked — not my claim to make).
- State proof status in public. A PARTIAL is called PARTIAL on the page.
- "No AI. No machine learning." True — say it. And never fake it the other way.

Calibrated honesty is the whole thing.
