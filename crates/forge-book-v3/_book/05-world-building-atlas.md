The World-Building Atlas

The one place to build toward. The world-building capability of this engine is deep and vast — two to three world builders' worth, plus a decade of folded game logic, all made agnostic. The problem was never missing capability. It was that the capability sat scattered across some twenty crates and stranded archives, with the seams between the organs left open. This atlas is the map: every organ, where it lives, whether it is wired, and the wires still to run. It was compiled from a fifteen-lane read-only sweep of the live repository and the archives on 2026-07-17.

The one-line truth: the organs are live; the seams are open. This is a wiring program, not a build-from-zero.

Status words used below. LIVE means the organ has a live caller. ORPHAN means real, compiling code with no caller yet. STRANDED means it sits in an archive, not in the live tree. FOLDED means it has already been merged into a live crate.

Spine and host, the Tauri union. The studio rides the forge-app-slapp Tauri spine — the kit_pane, binds, edicts, and vision commands proven this session. The kit registry forge_vix::loader::STUDIO_PANELS holds the servable surfaces; the world-building kits level_architect, world_node, canvas, canvas_window, create, create3d, and 3d are all registered and served. The Magic Canvas is forge-gui/src/windows/canvas.rs CanvasWindow — the artboard where one pixel is one vixel is one voxel, with F5 flipping 2D to 3D through the forge-render GlassCameraDirector (a SuperMaxAtom camera plus a 2D-to-3D DimTransition). This is the anchor: it has the camera and it has the physics.

Terrain and worldgen, all live. forge-zones/src/worldgen holds the prime-sieve Ulam-spiral voxel generator (folded from prime-sieve-worldgen). forge-game-systems/src/zone_heightmap.rs carries DestructibleTerrain, MaterialGrid, and TerrainObject. forge-zones/src/heightmap.rs is multi-octave Perlin with raise, lower, flatten, and smooth brushes. zone_terrain_math.rs holds thirteen archetype shape functions, integer-deterministic. forge-render/src/voxel_terrain.rs is marching-cubes isosurface extraction. And zone_level_bridge.rs generate_level takes a LevelConfig and returns a LoadedLevel — the live handler behind the level_architect edict.

Simulation and physics, live at 120 hertz. forge-studio/src/paint_host.rs tick_matter runs every deterministic-clock tick, driving the sf-wasm falling-sand and reaction simulation: matter (fire, smoke, gravity, flow), heat (conduction), electricity (charge), reactions (an alchemy table — lava plus water yields obsidian), and meteors (starfall). forge-gpu/src/vixel_pass.rs step_field is the canonical CPU automata; forge-shaders/src/vixel_automata.rs is the integer rule set with CPU parity. The orphans here are the GPU vixel_automata.wgsl (declared, not dispatched), sand_emerge.wgsl, and the sixteen-equation water-hammer in forge-physics/src/world/fluid.rs. The tunable gravity constant lives at forge-cart-brain/src/combat/projectile.rs as gravity_pmy, a permyriad ready for a slider.

Consequence, ecology, and declarative logic — the three named systems, all real. The World Consequence Engine is forge-consequence: a live physics-to-consequence dispatcher with curve tables (fire-on-wood, water-on-stone), a seven-by-seven mixture-of-experts fallback router, and sixteen-byte interaction queries producing typed consequences. Its open seam is upstream: nothing yet fires queries into it. forge-sieve/src/world.rs holds the ecology sieves — Land, Terrain, Weather, Moon, Ecology (a sixteen-population predation matrix), and Infection — all live, observing events and mutating state, but not yet emitting spawns. Astrakey is a two-lane system: a native Rust hot path (astrakey_sieve, Eratosthenes plus HMAC seeds, feeding a clingo-free reimplementation in asp_constraints.rs) and an authoring-time Python clingo bridge (tools/ironroot-py/sieve, with loot, level, and boss constraint programs). forge-book/src/asp.rs is the typed answer-set engine — grounding, fixpoint, integrity constraints, and .lp emission. Together they compose the ecology engine: the sieve generates, ASP validates, the consequence engine routes, the ecology sieves track state. It is roughly eighty percent present; the missing brick is spawn emission.

Actors, entities, and spawn. forge-game-systems/src/entity_store.rs is a generational, rollback-ready EntityStore — orphan, test-only. spawn.rs (SpawnManager, zone tables, respawn) and tick_engine.rs (a 120-hertz diff-ledger) are likewise orphan. The live actor container is arena_core/state.rs ArenaState, but its PlayerState and MobState are flat integer-millimetre structs with no unified position, velocity, collider, or AI split. cartridge.rs is live via the Worms sink. The decision is to reuse the in-repo EntityStore and converge ArenaState onto it as we go — no external ECS crate.

Creation and generation. forge-game-systems/src/creation is the folded ironroot-creation-engine — eleven modules, eight hundred thirty-five tests, a Draft-Preview-Locked ledger and deterministic NPC, item, and zone artifact generation. Its open seams, per its own wiring-gaps note, are the preview and export bridges and the scene and zone payloads. forge-furnace is the build-time compiler to the sealed .forge_reg format.

Items, loot, dialogue, quest, and faction — the deveraux MUD lineage. forge-items carries the DEC-001 mint chain and socket assembly. forge-game-systems holds loot_tables, zones (fourteen zones across four eras, each with a mood), quests (a six-state machine), and factions (eight factions, stance ranks, kill-effect cascades). forge-book holds the authored quest, faction, dialogue, inventory, loot, and ASP-linted greeter models. sf-wasm/src/mud.rs is the deveraux MUD world model itself: a Fibonacci room pool, twelve alchemical gates, the Tria Prima of salt, sulfur, and mercury, the central-third skill gate, and a hash-chained event ledger.

The world-builder surfaces — the two to three builders. Live and registered: level_architect, world_node, canvas, canvas_window, create3d. Stranded in the divmerge archive, their best ideas ready to be re-authored as VixiScript kits: the Cartographer (a heightmap canvas with nine brush tools and elemental and ecology biome palettes), the Atlas Sieve (a card-based pannable zone canvas with spring physics and exit-port string-dragging), the World Graph (typed nodes and twelve verbs with a Draft-Locked-Ledger lifecycle), the Build Level panel (a tile grid with a Wireframe-to-DragonFlame fidelity ladder), the Blueprint panel (a dungeon node-graph with traversal-aware edges), and the Animate panel — whose Toybox, Workbench, and Toolbox tiers are exactly the six-year-old, sixteen-year-old, and developer progressive-disclosure tiers the tri-tier UI calls for.

Harmonic and rhythm. forge-harmonics is deep — the rhythm judge with perfect, great, and good timing windows; a drum-and-bass sequencer at 174 beats per minute; Euclidean rhythm generation; drum-circle skeletons; a MIDI 2.0 codec; the music-speak semantic-audio path; sieve_harmonics mapping worldgen tier to ambient profile; and resonance_combat, where a player's pitch against a target's resonance produces a shatter. This session's vibe bus — rhythm to vibe channels to chrome — is the pre-cognitive ambient-rhythm delivery the ADHD-rhythm whitepapers call for.

Combat, abilities, supers, movement — deep and multi-lineage. Three lineages converge in the combat-alignment design: Python AstraKey, Rust ironroot, and JavaScript deveraux, plus the 13moons abilities, supers, and movement work. Live in forge-game-systems: the COM-001 resolver in combat/controller.rs, a spatial-grid hit lookup in combat/query.rs, combo scaling, the full damage-hit-crit-proc-mitigation pipeline in combat_math.rs, procedural weapon generation, and the dodge-parry-jump-gravity actor physics bridge. Live in forge-harmonics: resonance_combat and the rhythm judge. The hermetic layer defines seven stats — vigor, momentum, logic-depth, shadow-weight, tarnish, resonance, guilt — and dual elements: nine surface damage types and ten substrate reagents. Stranded in ironroot-edict: strike resolution, and input_chord — where the player's input is a musical chord, pitch and rhythm, evaluated against a target's resonance. The gap owed a targeted recovery: the AstraKey Python combat, and the 13moons abilities, supers, and movement, none yet crawled.

The forge-cartography question resolved. There is no single forge-cartography crate. The atlas already has a home and a seal, distributed across three complete live modules: forge-book/src/cartography.rs (a lore world-map that renders to a chapter), forge-core/src/world_map.rs (the runtime map with fog-of-war and axis-aligned bounds), and forge-calligraphy (signed glyph publishing with a SHA-256 provenance seal — the sealing mechanism for permanence). The authoring UI itself is partial and archived.

The richest stranded codebase is ironroot-edict, roughly a hundred and twenty files. Its recovery targets, not yet home: the photometric render engine (lighting and bloom per zone mood), the resonance-driven terrain waveform, the weather-to-audio-and-visual bridge, input_chord combat, the determinism replay-audit trail, the save-and-load persistence, the zone-JSON scene bridge, the zone renderer, and the full entity spawn tables. The live core is complete for the playable loop; these are the polish, persistence, and full-lore layers. Additional named logic sources for the recovery lane: adevstale (A Dev's Tale, a fourteen-ticket foundational program) and rundevrun.

The seams to close, brick by brick, each a proven-to-proven wire landed through the Tauri spine and proven on glass. Wire the level_generate edict to generate_level, rendering the level on the Magic Canvas artboard. Tick the live physics over that artboard and expose the gravity slider. Feed terrain and actor interactions into the consequence engine and apply its consequences back. Emit ecology spawns from the sieves and habitat rules into the reused entity store. Converge the actor state onto that store so actors live, tick, and despawn on the canvas. Re-author the stranded builders' best patterns as tri-tier VixiScript surfaces. Recover the ironroot-edict stranded tech as agnostic organs. Unify the three combat lineages and recover the AstraKey and 13moons layers. Fold the adevstale and rundevrun logic into the agnostic engine.

The doctrine, locked. Not a rush; foundational, brick by brick, each brick one proven wire. United with the Tauri development loop, not fighting it. Reuse first, and make new only as we find what is missing. Every surface authored in VixiScript, never a hand-rolled Rust panel. Integer-deterministic throughout — permyriad and milliunit, one binary, one 120-hertz clock. Every organ here was folded and made agnostic; treat each as a reusable primitive, never a game-specific fragment.
